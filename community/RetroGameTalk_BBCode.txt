[center][b]X-COM: ALIEN CONQUEST v1.0[/b]
Play the alien invasion[/center]

What if you commanded the invasion instead of X-COM? Alien Conquest is an unofficial, full alien-side campaign for X-COM: UFO Defense, built on OpenXcom Extended. You establish hidden ocean bases, direct UFO operations across Earth, recover people and resources, research human technology, and fight X-COM when its forces respond.

[b]What you do[/b]
• Build and expand underwater alien bases
• Recruit biological alien crew, equip UFOs, and deploy mechanical terror units as vehicles
• Choose Recon, Raid, and Terror orders based on the target
• Harvest rural resources, abduct humans in cities, and launch smaller Rural Terror Raids before heavier City Terror becomes available
• Research captured humans, weapons, equipment, and aircraft technology, then buy or fabricate the gear your invasion needs
• Fight X-COM on tactical maps when its bases detect and reach your operations; interceptors and base assaults add other threats
• Manage fuel, funding, monthly assessments, expansion, and a full win-or-lose campaign

[b]Requirements and installation[/b]
Windows is supported by the included executable. You need your own copy of X-COM: UFO Defense; its original data is not distributed with this release. Terror from the Deep data alone will not work.

1. Extract the game ZIP to a writable location, such as C:\AlienConquest
2. From your UFO Defense installation, copy GEODATA, GEOGRAPH, MAPS, ROUTES, SOUND, TERRAIN, UFOGRAPH, UFOINTRO, and UNITS directly into AlienConquest\UFO
3. Confirm the layout is like C:\AlienConquest\UFO\GEODATA
4. Run AlienConquest\openxcom.exe and start a new game

The game ZIP has a README.txt with a concise player guide and an HTML walkthrough with linked sections, research tables, and campaign details. A separate OpenXcom installation is not required.

[b]A few starting tips[/b]
Beginner is a good setting for a first campaign. Place your first base in open water near useful land targets. Use the Probe for Recon and put biological crew on the Scout before trying a Raid. Rural Raid becomes Harvest; City Raid becomes Abduction. Rural Terror can use an eligible light craft, while City Terror needs heavier preparation. Your early Purchase / Recruit list is intentionally short; research opens more orders and fabrication recipes. Recovered cargo reaches base stores only after its UFO returns home.
